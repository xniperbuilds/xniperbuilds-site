A TikTok downloader without watermark — as an app, not a website

Why that difference is the whole point.

Most ways to save a TikTok without the watermark are websites. You copy the link, leave the app, find a site, get past the pop-ups, wait for a countdown, tap the button that turns out to be an advert, and eventually a file lands in your Downloads folder — where your gallery cannot see it.

Riplox TT is an app, so none of that happens. Tap Share on the video, choose Riplox TT, and the file is in your gallery. No link to copy, no site to visit, no advert to dodge, no countdown. The watermark is gone because the app asks TikTok for the clean copy in the first place, not because anything is cropped or blurred afterwards.

Straight to the gallery, not to a Downloads folder

Saved videos go into your gallery where your camera roll lives, so they show up in Photos and can be shared onward immediately. Audio is saved as MP3 in your music folder. Nothing needs a file manager to find it afterwards.

It keeps going when you leave

Share several videos in a row and they queue. The download carries on with the app closed and the screen off — which is where most downloaders quietly stop — and each file notifies you as it lands.

What it does not do

It only handles TikTok. That is deliberate: one site, done properly, rather than a hundred handled badly. It has no ads, no account, no tracking, and asks for no permission it does not need. It cannot reach a private video that your own account cannot see. For Instagram there is a companion app, Riplox IG, built the same way; and for everything else — YouTube and a thousand other sites — there is Riplox Desktop on Windows.

On a PC instead?

If you want a TikTok video downloader for PC rather than a phone app, Riplox Desktop takes TikTok links alongside every other site, and downloads without the watermark in the same way. You can also share a TikTok from your phone to your PC and it downloads there — even if the PC is switched off at the time.

Questions people ask first

Does it really remove the TikTok watermark?

The saved file has no watermark, because the app requests the clean version rather than editing one out. Nothing is cropped, blurred or covered, so the video keeps its full frame and original quality.

Is it free, and are there ads?

Free, with no ads anywhere in it, no paid tier and no account to create. It is open source, so the code that does all of this can be read by anyone.

Where do saved videos go?

Into your gallery, alongside your camera roll, so they appear in Photos and can be shared straight on. MP3 audio goes to your music folder. You do not need a file manager to go looking for anything.

Does the download stop if I close the app?

No. It carries on with the app closed and the screen off, and tells you as each file lands. Sharing several videos in a row queues them rather than losing them.

Is it on Google Play?

It is distributed as an APK from GitHub rather than through Play. Every release publishes a SHA-256 checksum so you can confirm the file is the one we built. Android will ask you to allow installing from that source the first time.

Can it save a private or friends-only video?

No. It can only reach what your own account can already see, and it will say so rather than failing quietly.

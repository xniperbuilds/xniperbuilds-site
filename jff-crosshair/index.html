Your crosshair.
Your rules.

JFF Crosshair Pro puts a fully custom crosshair overlay on top of any mobile shooter — 160 styles, or your own image, placed exactly on your aim point. It starts with your game, adjusts without leaving it, and does not disappear mid-match. Aim trainer, reaction test, tap-speed test and ping checker built in.

Download APK View on GitHub

Free · Android 8.0+ · No account

Aim like you mean it

A crosshair you control — and one that is still there in round three.

160 crosshair styles

20 shapes × 8 variants — crosses, circles, chevrons, brackets, sniper scopes and dots.

Use your own image

Any PNG with a transparent background becomes your crosshair — size, fade and position still apply.

Fully customizable

Size, thickness, gap, center dot, opacity, outline, 16 swatches + full RGB.

It stays on screen

Most crosshair apps quietly vanish mid-match. This one runs as a proper foreground service — on is on, until you turn it off.

Auto-start with your game

Pick your games once. The crosshair appears when the game opens and hides the moment you leave.

Adjust it mid-game

Open the panel from your notification and change size, fade and colour without leaving the game.

Exact positioning

Drag it with your finger using centre guides, or nudge it by exactly 1, 5 or 10 pixels.

On and off in one tap

A Quick Settings tile and a home-screen shortcut, so you never have to open the app to switch it.

Share your crosshair

Send a friend your exact setup as a short code, or paste theirs and get the same one.

Aim trainer

30-second target drills in three difficulties, with your best score saved.

Reaction & tap speed

Reaction test in milliseconds and a 10-second CPS test to warm up your fingers.

Ping checker

Check your connection quality before you queue — know it's game-ready.

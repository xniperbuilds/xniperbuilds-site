Privacy Policy

xniperbuilds.com · Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy covers the xniperbuilds.com website. Each XniperBuilds app and program has its own privacy policy, linked at the bottom of this page, because each one handles different things.

The short version: there are no accounts and no ads on this website. We use Google Analytics to see how the site is used. If you fill in one of our forms or email us, we receive what you send, and we use it only to reply to you or to handle what you asked for. We do not sell data to anyone.

Analytics

Every page uses Google Analytics. It tells us which pages are visited, how people arrive, and the general type of device and browser, along with an approximate location that Google works out from your IP address. It uses cookies to do this. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can block it with your browser’s settings or with Google’s opt-out add-on, and the site keeps working either way.

Forms

Our forms are delivered to our email inbox by a form service called Web3Forms. Each form sends only what you type into it:

  • App tester application — your name, the email you use on Google Play, an optional second way to reach you, your phone model, and any notes.
  • Bug report or feedback — which app, what happened, and optionally your phone model and an email if you want a reply.
  • Custom software enquiry — your name, email, an optional WhatsApp number, and your project details.
  • Xniper Social Studio order — your name, email, country, an optional WhatsApp number, and any note.

We use these details only to reply, to add you to the testing programme you asked to join, or to deliver what you ordered.

Email

If you write to hello@xniperbuilds.com, we receive your email address and your message, and use them only to reply.

Other services your browser talks to

  • Google Fonts — the site’s fonts load from Google, which receives your IP address as part of that request.
  • GitHub — download buttons point to our releases on GitHub. To show the latest version number and file size next to a button, your browser asks GitHub’s public API; nothing about you is added to that request.
  • Web3Forms — only when you send a form, as described above.

How long we keep it

We keep form submissions and emails only as long as we need them to handle your request — for testers, while you are in the testing programme. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what you have sent us, email hello@xniperbuilds.com and we will take care of it.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

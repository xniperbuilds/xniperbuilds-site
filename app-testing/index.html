How it works

Four steps, and only the third one asks anything of you.

1

Send us your Play Store email

Use the form below — it has to be the Google account signed into the Play Store on the phone you will test on.

2

Accept the invite

We add you to the tester list and send back a private join link. One tap, then install from Google Play like any other app.

3

Keep it for 14 days

Google needs 12 testers running the app for 14 continuous days before it can go public. Uninstalling early can reset that clock for everyone, so leave it on the phone.

4

Tell us what broke

A screenshot or a single line is plenty. If nothing goes wrong, you do not have to send anything at all.

Questions testers ask

Does testing cost anything?

No. Nothing to pay, no subscription, no card. The apps you test are free, and so is the programme.

Why do you need my Gmail address?

Google Play closed testing works by email. Your address goes on the tester list for the app, which is what makes the private install link work on your phone. It has to be the exact Google account signed into the Play Store on the device you will test on.

What does "free access to paid apps" mean?

When we release an app or a pro unlock that other people pay for, testers get it at no cost on the Google account they test with. It stays free for that account — it is not a trial and not a discount code.

Why 14 days?

Google requires an app to run a closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 continuous days before it can be published to everyone. If people uninstall early the clock can reset, which is why we ask you to leave it on the phone for the two weeks.

Do I have to write reports?

No. A screenshot or a one-line message when something looks wrong is plenty. If nothing breaks, you do not have to send anything at all.

Can I leave the programme?

Yes, any time. Uninstall the app and reply once to say you are out. There is no lock-in and nothing to cancel.

Will a test build damage my phone?

No. Test builds install from Google Play like any other app, run in their own sandbox and uninstall the same way. They are the same builds we run on our own phones.

Found a bug? Want a feature?

Tester or not — if one of our apps misbehaves, or you want it to do something it doesn't, tell us here.

Prefer email? hello@xniperbuilds.com

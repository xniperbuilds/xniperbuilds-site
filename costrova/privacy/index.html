Privacy Policy

Costrova · Last updated: 12 November 2026

Costrova is a project expense app for Android, published by XniperBuilds. It keeps the money side of your projects — expenses, payments, money received, parties and budgets — and turns it into reports. Everything runs on your own phone. This policy explains exactly what the app stores and what leaves your phone.

The short version: No account, no sign-up and no server. Your projects, entries, parties, receipt photos and report settings are stored only on your device and never reach us. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob; Costrova Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play and removes the ads. Those two are the only parts of the app that talk to anyone else.

Data we collect

We collect nothing ourselves. There is no login, no registration and no user profile, and we have no server that receives your data. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, location or usage analytics.

The one exception is the ads in the free version: Google AdMob, which serves them, uses your device's advertising ID. That is described under Advertising below.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your projects — name, client, type, currency, contract value, budget and notes.
  • Your entries — expenses, payments and money received, with amounts, dates, categories, quantities, rates and notes.
  • Parties you add — suppliers, contractors, labour and clients, with the name and any phone number or note you type in.
  • Receipt photos you attach to entries.
  • Your report template — business name, logo, colour, layout, footer note and watermark.
  • Your settings, such as theme, default currency, backup reminder and app lock.

All of this lives in the app's private storage and is removed when you uninstall the app — which is why the app reminds you to keep a backup.

Backups and reports

A backup is a single file containing everything listed above, including receipt photos and your logo. The app writes it only where you choose — a folder on your phone, or an app you pick from the share menu — and never sends it anywhere on its own. Restoring reads a backup file you pick; before it replaces anything, the app keeps a copy of the current data on the phone so you can switch back.

With Costrova Pro you can turn on Auto backup, which writes one backup a day into a folder on your phone that you choose, and keeps the last three.

PDF, Excel and CSV reports are created on your phone. They are shared or saved only when you tap Share or Save.

If Android's own backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in that backup, as it does for other apps. That is a phone setting managed by Google; you can turn it off in Android Settings.

Permissions

  • Internet — used only to load ads in the free version and to talk to Google Play for the Pro purchase. Your ledger never goes online.
  • Fingerprint, face or PIN (app lock, optional) — when you turn on App lock, Android asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock. The check is done by Android; the app only learns whether it succeeded and never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN.
  • Receipt photos — taken with your phone's camera app or picked with Android's photo picker. The app receives only the one photo you choose and needs no camera or storage permission.

Advertising

The free version of Costrova is funded by ads served through Google AdMob. You will see:

  • a banner on the Projects, Parties and Reports lists;
  • at most one full-screen ad after you share or save a report — never on your first report, and no more than once every ten minutes.

There is no ad on the home screen, none when you open the app, and none while you are adding or saving an entry. Costrova Pro removes every ad, and the ad service is not started at all.

To serve those ads, Google AdMob uses your device's resettable advertising ID, along with technical information such as your device type, IP address and rough region, and it may use that to personalise ads. This is handled by Google, not by us — we never see it, and it is not linked to any identity, because the app has no accounts. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), Google's consent form is shown first, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy options for ads. Google's use of the data is covered by the Google Privacy & Terms.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, which also lets you turn off ad personalisation. The app keeps working either way.

Purchases

Costrova Pro is a one-time purchase — there is no subscription and nothing renews by itself. It unlocks unlimited projects, Excel export, auto backup, extra report layouts and the watermark, and removes the ads.

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card, your billing address or your Google account; the app only asks Google Play whether Pro has been bought, so it comes back after a reinstall or on a new phone signed into the same account. We have no server and no record of any purchase. Refunds and payment questions go through Google Play, and Google's handling of that data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Third parties

Google AdMob (with Google's consent tool) and Google Play Billing are the only third-party services in the app. There is no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no third-party account system. We do not sell or share your data with anyone, because we do not collect any.

Children

Costrova is a business tool for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

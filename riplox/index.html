Your phone finds it. Your PC keeps it.

Even when the PC is switched off at the time.

Most of what is worth keeping gets found on a phone and wanted on a computer. The usual answer is to message the link to yourself and deal with it later, which mostly means never.

Riplox puts a postbox between the two. Share a link from your phone and it joins a queue. If the PC is on, the download starts in seconds. If the PC is off or asleep, the link waits — and everything that piled up downloads the moment Riplox is running again. Eleven links shared overnight means eleven files waiting in the morning.

No same Wi-Fi, no pairing dance every time, nothing to leave open at either end. The link is encrypted on the phone and only your PC can read it; the relay in between carries a sealed envelope it cannot open.

Which one should I install?

On Windows, Riplox Desktop covers everything — a thousand-plus sites including TikTok and Instagram — so it is the only one most people need. On a phone, the general Riplox app handles most sites, while Riplox TT and Riplox IG each do one site properly, from the share sheet, with no link to copy. Installing more than one is fine; they do not interfere with each other.

Privacy you can hold

Strong encryption wrapped in things that are actually fun to use — all on-device.

AES-256 lock

Password-lock any text with AES-256-GCM. Only the right password opens it.

Disguise & hide

Turn secrets into innocent emojis, or hide them in invisible ink no one can see.

Fingerprint vault

Keep encrypted notes behind biometrics or a PIN — locked and out of sight.

File locker

Wrap whole files in an encrypted container you can store or share safely.

QR & decode

Generate and scan QR codes, Morse and binary — paste anything and it auto-decodes.
